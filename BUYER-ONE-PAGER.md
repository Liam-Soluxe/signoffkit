# SignoffKit

## The problem

Small agencies and freelancers often manage launch QA and scope changes through spreadsheets, screenshots and email. The work may be checked, but the final evidence is scattered and the approval boundary is unclear.

## The product

SignoffKit turns that loose material into one portable review pack:

- Structured templates for websites, campaigns, creative delivery and scope changes
- Pass, flag, pending and not-applicable decisions
- Reviewer notes and attached evidence
- Browser-calculated SHA-256 file digests
- Versioned JSON for later editing
- Self-contained HTML for handover, printing and archiving

No account is required. Files are not uploaded by SignoffKit.

## Ideal buyer

- Two to ten person marketing, design or web agency
- Freelancer delivering fixed-scope client work
- Fractional marketer responsible for launch readiness
- Small internal team that needs a repeatable handover record

## Why it is different

Full proofing suites centre on collaborative review platforms, seats and storage. SignoffKit is a small local-first utility for the final QA and evidence-pack step. It does not replace contracts, e-signatures or project management.

## Current beta

The public beta is free and open source. It tests whether users repeatedly create and share evidence packs before hosted collaboration or payment is justified.

## Potential commercial layer, only after validation

- Saved team templates and naming rules
- Hosted no-login client review links
- White-labelled report themes
- Shared pack history and immutable server timestamps
- Agency workspaces and controlled retention

## Validation threshold

Do not build the hosted layer until at least one of these is true:

- Five unrelated buyers pay for a hosted pack or project pass
- Three teams return for a second real project within 30 days
- Ten agencies request the same hosted collaboration feature

## Boundaries

A generated pack is not an electronic signature, legal approval, compliance certificate or cryptographic timestamp. It records evidence and review decisions supplied by the user.
