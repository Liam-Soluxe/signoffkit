# SignoffKit opportunity evidence

## Current public signals

1. A freelancer described creating a hidden website approval form with proofreading, error and additional-fee acknowledgement checkboxes because normal sign-off was inadequate: https://www.reddit.com/r/freelance/comments/vnew7r/how_to_get_clients_to_sign_off/
2. A freelancer asking how to prove retainer work described exporting time data and providing screenshots and status updates: https://www.reddit.com/r/freelance/comments/iyzys9/how_to_show_work_completed_against_retainer/
3. Marketing QA guidance still commonly recommends checklists and Quality Workbooks to standardise and document review: https://gosolomon.com/qa-digital-marketing/
4. PageProof positions proof checklists as an additional traceability layer before final approval: https://pageproof.com/learn/marketing-and-brand-compliance-with-proof-checklists
5. Current approval-tool comparisons repeatedly note that enterprise proofing suites can be excessive for smaller teams: https://feedbucket.app/blog/client-approval-software
6. Agency review guidance identifies version, timestamp and named sign-off trails as protection against later approval disputes: https://ybug.io/blog/review-and-approval-software-agencies

## Opportunity

A local-first, no-account evidence-pack generator occupies a narrower position than a full proofing platform. It packages the workflow small teams already assemble manually without requiring them to upload client work or adopt another project-management system.

## Why this first

- Adjacent to Soluxe's operating experience.
- Useful without integrations or credentials.
- Static hosting means near-zero operating burden.
- Can be demonstrated with synthetic data.
- Generic engine is separable from future agency-specific templates.
- Clear expansion path exists only if real usage warrants it.

## Evidence limitations

This is problem evidence, not proof of willingness to pay. The public beta is designed to test activation and sharing before hosted collaboration or billing is built.
