# SignoffKit competitor and positioning note

## Existing categories

### Full online proofing suites

- GoVisually: creative review and approval, starting from a low per-user plan with broader collaboration, storage and versioning. Source: https://govisually.com/pricing
- PageProof: broad proofing workflows, storage, versioning and enterprise controls, with its published Team plan positioned at USD 249 per month. Source: https://pageproof.com/pricing
- Pastel: visual feedback canvases for websites and creative work, with paid team plans. Source: https://usepastel.com/plans

### Lightweight approval and sign-off

- ClientProof: hosted no-login client approvals, shared project links and timestamped decisions. Source: https://clientproof.app/features/client-approval-software/
- BesTest UAT generator: free browser-local UAT sign-off document generation. Source: https://getbestest.com/tools/uat-sign-off-sheet-generator/

### Local-first evidence packs

- Proof Pack: local-first proof packets for completed work. Source: https://github.com/reesepj/proof-pack
- Tracepack: local-first evidence collection, privacy review and export. Source: https://github.com/ace2016/tracepack

## Deliberate wedge

SignoffKit is not another hosted feedback portal, project-management suite, generic evidence archive or electronic-signature product. Its initial wedge is:

> A browser-local launch QA desk for agencies that combines reusable marketing and website checklists with evidence hashing, blocker scoring and a self-contained review pack.

## What the beta must prove

1. Users value structured launch checks more than a blank document generator.
2. Evidence attachments and blocker scoring improve the handoff enough to repeat.
3. Users export or share the finished pack.
4. A marketing-specific template library provides a credible acquisition wedge.

## What is intentionally absent

- Hosted approval links
- Electronic signatures
- Legal or compliance claims
- Team accounts
- Client data storage
- Integrations
- Billing

These are not missing MVP features. They are future hypotheses that require usage evidence before adding operational burden.
